Preamble

1. Nature of Engagement

1.1 Remote.Workr™ is an outsourcing platform that connects clients with remote workers globally. Workers are engaged as independent contractors, not employees. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the Platform and the Worker.

1.2 The Platform reserves the right to assign, modify, or withdraw tasks at any time without prior notice. Task availability depends on client demand, subscription tier, and platform capacity.

2. Eligibility & Registration

  • You must be at least 18 years of age to register on the Platform.
  • You must provide accurate, current, and complete information during registration.
  • You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
  • One account per individual is permitted. Multiple accounts will result in permanent suspension.
  • The Platform reserves the right to verify your identity and reject incomplete or fraudulent applications.

3. Subscription Plans & Token Requirements

3.1 Free Trial & Basic Plans are available at no cost and do not require any goCOINS wallet balance. These plans carry limited task availability, earning potential, and time-bound access at the Platform's discretion.

3.2 Agent Plan requires a minimum balance of 1,300 goCOINS tokens in your registered wallet at the time of activation. This balance is not a fee but a prerequisite to unlock premium task access.

3.3 Table Kings Plan requires a minimum balance of 2,500 goCOINS tokens in your registered wallet. This tier grants access to the highest-paying tasks, exclusive bonuses, AirDrops, AirPops, and Giveaway Dash events.

3.4 Token requirements may be updated by the Platform with 7 days' advance notice to registered workers.

4. Task Completion & Quality Standards

  • All assigned tasks must be completed within the specified deadline.
  • Workers must maintain a minimum task completion quality score as defined by each client's brief.
  • Submission of fraudulent, plagiarized, or AI-generated work (where prohibited) may result in reward forfeiture and account suspension.
  • The Platform reserves the right to reject task submissions that do not meet quality standards without obligation to compensate.
  • Workers are expected to communicate any inability to complete assigned tasks promptly.

5. Rewards, Payments & Earnings

5.1 Rewards are disbursed in the form of airtime, internet data bundles, and/or goCOINS tokens depending on your active subscription plan and task type.

5.2 Earnings are credited to your platform wallet within 24–72 hours of verified task completion. The Platform is not liable for delays caused by third-party payment processors, telecommunications providers, or blockchain network congestion.

5.3 Bonuses, AirDrops, AirPops, and Giveaway Dash rewards are discretionary, time-limited, and exclusive to qualifying Table Kings subscribers. These are not guaranteed income and may be withdrawn or modified at any time.

5.4 Workers are solely responsible for any applicable taxes on earnings from the Platform in their respective jurisdictions.

6. Prohibited Conduct

  • Spamming, bot-generated submissions, or use of automated tools to complete tasks
  • Creating multiple accounts to circumvent plan restrictions
  • Sharing login credentials with third parties
  • Attempting to reverse-engineer, hack, or manipulate the Platform's systems
  • Engaging in harassment, fraudulent activity, or misrepresentation
  • Disclosing confidential client information obtained through task assignments

Violation of any prohibited conduct clause will result in immediate account termination and potential legal action.

All work produced by the Worker in the completion of Platform tasks is considered work-for-hire and the exclusive intellectual property of the respective client unless otherwise stated in the task brief. The Worker waives any moral rights to such work upon submission and acceptance.

Your information will be used solely for platform operations, task assignment, reward disbursement, and compliance purposes. Data will not be sold to third parties without your explicit consent.

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time. The Platform may suspend or terminate your account with or without cause and without prior notice if you violate any term of this Agreement. Upon termination, all pending earned rewards that have been verified prior to termination will be honored, subject to review.
